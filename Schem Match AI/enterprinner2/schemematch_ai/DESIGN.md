## Brand & Style

The design system merges authoritative institutional credibility with modern algorithmic precision and radical accessibility. Designed for underserved micro-entrepreneurs, rural business owners, and small enterprise founders across India, the interface instills sovereign trust while eliminating bureaucratic intimidation.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Corporate Modern with High-Legibility Inclusivity**:
- **Trust & Authority**: Deep, unyielding sovereign blue provides the gravitational core, signaling national-grade stability, bank security, and official compliance.
- **Empowerment & Clarity**: Vibrant, hopeful teal accents guide the user toward actionable financial matches, subsidies, and credit schemes.
- **Cognitive Ease**: Generous structural spacing, ultra-clean contrast, plain-language visual cues, multilingual indicators, and multimodal assistive touchpoints (including AI voice prompts) lower digital literacy barriers without feeling patronizing.

## Colors

The color palette anchors high-stakes financial and government entitlement decisions through high-contrast legibility and purposeful signaling:

- **Primary (`#0F2B5C`)**: Deep Sovereign Navy. Represents institutional stability, statutory rigor, and foundational authority. Used for prominent headers, active navigation rails, key structural banners, and high-impact calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488`)**: AI Precision Teal. Symbolizes forward movement, technological intelligence, and modern algorithmic assistance. Used for conversational AI prompts, secondary actions, progress milestones, and voice wave accents.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Grant & Match Emerald. Reserved explicitly for positive financial verification: "Eligible Schemes," "Disbursement Approved," "100% Match," and subsidy success states.
- **Neutrals**:
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFFFF` for elevated interactive cards.
  - `Surface Muted`: `#F8FAFC` for screen canvases and background containment.
  - `Border Soft`: `#E2E8F0` for structural divisions and high-yield form outlines.
  - `Text Neutral`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for primary headings and critical readouts; `#334155` (Slate 700) for high-contrast body copy complying with strict WCAG 2.1 AAA visibility standards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural and motivational headings with **Inter** for dense transactional interfaces, forms, and multilingual localized strings:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: The sculpted apertures and friendly geometric curves eliminate administrative coldness, presenting complex policies and funding initiatives as approachable opportunities.
- **Body & Data (Inter)**: Delivers unmatched legibility at small sizes, optimal vertical proportioning for Hindi and regional scripts, and unambiguous numerical rendering for financial figures and subsidy amounts.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for rupee values, match percentages, and eligibility scores to guarantee scannability in comparative tables.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8px base rhythm powers the interface layout, prioritizing rapid field navigation across budget mobile devices and desktop verification dashboards:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and a maximum content constrain of `80rem` (1280px). Utilizes a persistent left-rail navigation pane for rapid scheme browsing, profile completeness tracking, and quick language switching.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Interactive side panels collapse into contextual modal drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins. Critical interactive controls (Voice input, Eligibility Wizard actions, Apply triggers) adhere to an ergonomic thumb zone with a sticky bottom action bar.
- **Form Density**: Fields use generous touch heights (minimum 48px, ideally 52px) to prevent mis-taps by users navigating on entry-level smartphones with screen protectors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines clean tonal surfaces, low-contrast boundaries, and soft navy-tinted ambient shadows to retain lightness while establishing solid physical affordances:

- **Surface Levels**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: Flat `#F8FAFC`. Unbroken backdrop for systemic content flow.
  - `Level 1 (Default Card)`: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with no shadow, keeping screen noise low during high data volume.
  - `Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Cards)`: `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a dual shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 43, 92, 0.05), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 43, 92, 0.07)`.
  - `Level 3 (Modals, Voice AI Drawers, Language Pickers)`: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(15, 43, 92, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 43, 92, 0.04)`.
- **Match Focus**: Cards with an eligibility score exceeding 90% receive a distinct 1.5px border tint in tertiary emerald (`#10B981`) and a soft diffused accent glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system implements a balanced **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry, cultivating warmth without sacrificing bureaucratic seriousness:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to input fields, buttons, table cell groupings, and standard chips.
- **Card & Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to scheme cards, AI conversation tiles, document verification boxes, and stat widgets.
- **Surface Overlays (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px)**: Applied to voice interaction pods, modal sheets, and key hero banners.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status indicators, dynamic language selection tags, and categorical subsidy pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Sovereign Navy (`#0F2B5C`) with white label, 50px height, 8px radius, subtle active state scale (`0.98`). Designed to feel authoritative and decisive (e.g., "Submit Application", "Check Eligibility").
- **Secondary (AI / Assisted)**: Teal gradient or solid surface (`#0D9488`) with high-contrast white text, used for AI matching, document autocompletion, and voice prompts.
- **Tertiary & Ghost**: Transparent background with 1px border in `#CBD5E1` and Slate 800 typography for ancillary pathways like "Save Scheme for Later" or "Download Guidelines (PDF)".

### Scheme Match Cards
- Structured white cards with a top-weighted "Match Meter" badge (e.g., "96% Direct Match" in `#059669` tertiary tone).
- Incorporates institutional badges (e.g., "MSME Ministry", "NABARD", "State Sponsored") with clear rupee figures rendered in bold tabular tabular type.
- Built-in multi-lingual audio button allowing illiterate or non-English literate users to tap for a 30-second localized summary.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Distinct 52px height for mobile accessibility.
- Inactive state: White fill with a clean 1.5px border (`#CBD5E1`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to Sovereign Navy (`#0F2B5C`) reinforced with a 3px ambient ring (`rgba(15, 43, 92, 0.15)`).
- Visual validation: Instant inline icon feedback (Checkmark in `#059669` or contextual assist in `#0D9488`).

### AI Voice Interaction Waveforms & Multimodal Assist
- Floating action widget with an organic pulsing ring utilizing secondary teal (`#0D9488`) and sovereign navy (`#0F2B5C`).
- Real-time soundwave indicators built using dynamic vertical bars (varying heights 4px to 28px) to visually affirm that vernacular voice queries in Marathi, Tamil, Hindi, or Bengali are being actively parsed.

### Badges, Chips & Language Selectors
- **Language Chips**: Fixed top-bar selector with native script representation (e.g., "English | हिंदी | தமிழ்") wrapped in high-visibility pill buttons.
- **Scheme Tags**: Soft-tinted surface tags (e.g., `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.1)` with `#0D9488` text for "Women Entrepreneur Subsidy").
